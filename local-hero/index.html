One passport, every city we know. Each page is a place a local would take you. Get it stamped and the city stops treating you like a visitor.

How it works

Three steps. No tour guide.

  1. 01

    Get the passport.

    It’s free. It fits in a back pocket. It opens doors that don’t have signs.

  2. 02

    Go where we say.

    Every page is a place a local would take you: the café before the queue, the bar nobody lists. Times in 24h. Directions included.

  3. 03

    Get stamped.

    Show it at the door. They stamp it. Every day in the passport has two pages: a light one for the day (the café, the park, the market nobody posts) and a dark one for the night (the bar, the club, the door with no sign). Fill both and you stop being a tourist.
